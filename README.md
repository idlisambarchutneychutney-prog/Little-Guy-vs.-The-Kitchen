## Little Guy vs. Marshmallow: Kitchen Showdown
Welcome to Little Guy vs. Marshmallow: Kitchen Showdown! This is a chaotic, fast-paced local 2-player battle game built for Jame Gam—and it's the very first game I've ever made!
Dive onto the kitchen floor, battle it out with your opponent, and fight to claim the ultimate prize: the legendary Big Double Chunk Chocolate Cookie.

## Controls
Little Guy (Player 1)
Move: W, A, S, D
Attack: R

Marshmallow (Player 2)
Move: Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right)
Attack: L (Strikes with knife when within 250 pixels)

## Game Rules & Features
HP Tracking: Both characters start with 4 Health Points (HP).
Combat: Get close to your opponent (within 250 pixels) and press your attack key to deal damage and swap your sprite to your attack weapon.
Victory: The first player to drop their opponent's HP from 4 down to 0 wins instantly and triggers the win screen to claim their cookie!

## Built With
Engine: Godot 4.7.2
